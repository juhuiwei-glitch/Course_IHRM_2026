# Kaffeegarten: Some Are Thinking About Leaving

Kaffeegarten is a growing chain of coffee shops known for good coffee and a comfortable atmosphere. Rather than competing primarily on price or speed, the company emphasizes high-quality beverages, a welcoming atmosphere, and friendly interactions between employees and customers. The company wants its stores to be places where customers can meet friends, work, or simply stay and enjoy their time. At the same time, Kaffeegarten has always encouraged customers to personalize their drinks.

About three months ago, the company introduced an updated digital ordering system. Customers can now customize their drinks in many ways before placing an order through the app. They can change the type of milk, number of espresso shots, amount of syrup, sweetness, temperature, toppings, and several other options. The company hopes that the new system will make ordering more convenient and allow customers to receive drinks that better match their personal preferences.

However, at a recent meeting attended by store managers and representatives from headquarters, an HR manager became aware of a growing concern. Several store managers reported similar problems: customer complaints had increased, and some experienced employees had expressed an intention to leave.

The HR manager decided to look into the situation in more detail and gathered further information from Maya, who manages one of Kaffeegarten’s busiest stores. Her team includes several baristas, including Ethan and Leo, who have both worked at the store for more than three years, and Nina, who joined the company five months ago. Ethan has always been one of the strongest performers in the store and can usually prepare drinks quickly and accurately. Leo is also experienced and is especially good at interacting with customers. He often remembers regular customers, chats with them while they wait, and helps create the kind of welcoming atmosphere that encourages people to stay. Although Leo is excellent with customers, he has always preferred interacting with people to working with complicated digital systems.

Nina is still developing her skills as a barista and occasionally needs help from her colleagues, especially when preparing more complicated drinks. This has not normally been a problem. The team has a friendly atmosphere, and Ethan, Leo, and the other baristas are usually happy to help one another. Maya has always felt that these positive working relationships also contribute to the atmosphere customers experience in the store.

Over the past three months, however, Maya has noticed several changes. Customer complaints have increased, especially during busy periods. Some customers have received the wrong milk, syrup, or number of espresso shots. Others have complained about long waiting times. Maya has also noticed that employees appear more rushed and spend less time talking with customers.

At first, Maya was especially concerned about Nina because she was relatively new. However, she gradually noticed that even Ethan and Leo sometimes made mistakes when the store became busy. On some mornings, many mobile orders arrived within a few minutes, and several of them contained five or six modifications. Employees had to pay close attention to the details of each order while also helping colleagues when difficulties arose.

Maya has also noticed that Leo, who was once one of the most cheerful people behind the counter, now often looks serious and rushed during busy periods. Leo has told Maya that the atmosphere at work feels very different from before and that he is considering moving to a smaller café. Nina has also mentioned that she might look for a job at another café once she becomes more confident in her coffee-making skills.

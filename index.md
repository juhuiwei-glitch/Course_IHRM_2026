## High-Performance Work System

Organizational performance does not depend on a single factor. What matters is how well the different parts of the organization work together. This idea is central to the concept of a <span class="key-term">high-performance work system (HPWS)</span>.

When HR practices such as <span class="key-term">job design</span>, <span class="key-term">training</span>, and <span class="key-term">compensation</span> reinforce the same priorities, the organization is more likely to create a coherent system.

<div class="section-number" id="performance-management">02</div>

## Performance Management

<span class="larger-key-term">Performance management</span> helps connect employee behavior and performance with organizational goals.

<span class="label">Strategic purpose</span>

Performance measures should reflect what the organization is trying to achieve and make clear what employees are expected to contribute.

<span class="label">Administrative purpose</span>

Performance information can support decisions about pay, promotion, retention, reassignment, and termination.

<span class="label">Developmental purpose</span>

Performance management can identify strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
